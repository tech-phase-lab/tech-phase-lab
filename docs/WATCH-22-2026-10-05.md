## Limits

- Earnings commentary is saved and reviewed, not automatically regenerated after future releases. Each page discloses this under Sources & updates.
- Live monitor consumes the existing published news API; this rollout does not change ingestion, source coverage, translation or source-to-publication latency. No claim of complete event capture.
- Twelve Data is not connected. Quotes remain existing delayed TradingView embeds. SK hynix uses KRX:000660; browser verification showed the provider does not offer this symbol in its embedded widget, so its compact price area links directly to the provider instead.
- Paid cards are omitted from the server payload for free, signed-out, unavailable and expired memberships. No access policy change.
- Forecasts and contracts are never described as delivered revenue. No buy/sell ratings or fabricated insider-sale allegations.
