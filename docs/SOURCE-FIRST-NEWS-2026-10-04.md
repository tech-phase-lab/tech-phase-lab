# Source-first news admission

The news feed now admits an approved, intact retained source for the existing
materiality assessment even when it has no tracked ticker or several companies.
Ticker metadata is optional enrichment. No synthetic equity identity is created;
source-news jobs use a null actor ticker, source-derived titles, and an empty or
source-supported ticker list. Existing single-company publication routes keep
priority and existing semantic review records are not reset.

The retained source/head/body/clock, correction, promotion, duplicate, batch and
shared-call-budget checks remain in place. Acquisition queries, provider settings,
stream routing and limits are unchanged. The worker still makes one bounded
materiality/writing assessment on its normal schedule and budget.

Generic source news preserves complete evidence units and checks literal names,
source-led actors, status, numbers, conditions, negation and existing factual
anchors. These are conservative contradiction guards, not a general entailment
proof. Unsupported factual output remains private; being an untracked company
is not an exclusion reason.

A bounded optional parser recognizes explicit backing plus a subject's product
release and historical signed compute/supply/licensing agreements. Names are
source data. This graph is not required for admission. Where it fully covers all
source claims, the application renders the graph after a positive materiality
decision, retaining the exact original response in a private derivation audit.
Public reads freshly derive the source graph and require the matching source,
publication hash, original decision and successful shared-ledger call. Wording
variants therefore do not trigger a second call or an indefinite wording hold.
Unparsed amounts, dates, conditions or clauses cannot be dropped into that
renderer; they use the ordinary complete-source factual path.

Independent-review hardening: generic source units with passive acquisition
roles, coordinated independent actor clauses or multiple material noun-count
pairs stay private as unproven-source-relation. The supported simple permit
condition binds if versus unless and approval versus denial separately in each
language; unrecognized conditions stay private as unproven-source-condition.
These bounded holds do not apply to the fully derived Reflection claim graph.
A completed same-source job and successful ledger call prevent paid regeneration
even if both its derivation audit and publication are missing or damaged. New
source revisions retain their normal eligibility. Valid empty event associations
are accepted without rewriting that metadata. Optional public ticker enrichment
is capped at the existing transport limit of five; private source facts remain
complete and the story is not discarded for tag overflow.

A second review tightened that boundary at the clause level: generic source
negation, unparsed coordination, comma clauses, additional sentences and
semicolon clauses now remain private unless handled by a fully typed source
renderer. The independently parsed permit condition is not counted as an
unbound main clause. Passive acquisitions are recognized by their be/get
auxiliary and participle within the clause, including temporal/adverb adjuncts
and omitted agents, rather than requiring an adjacent "acquired by" phrase.
Tests include thirteen passive variants, active controls, and secondary actors
introduced by descriptive noun phrases or bare comma clauses. This deliberately
bounded source-news grammar is not a universal natural-language verifier.
