Scope and service

Tech Phase Research provides news, earnings coverage and company analysis based on public information about U.S. companies. Planned PRO features include valuation scenarios, stock comparison, weekly research, commentary, shared member Q&A and notifications.

Accounts

Provide accurate registration information and protect your sign-in credentials. Do not share your account or impersonate another person.

Pricing, renewal and cancellation

Planned PRO prices are JPY 2,980/month (tax included) for Japan and US$20/month internationally. Monthly renewal from the subscription date and access through the paid period after cancellation are intended. International billing currency and taxes, procedures and deadlines will be finalized with payment integration. No subscriptions or payments are currently accepted.

Refunds and service issues

Refund terms and handling of duplicate charges or prolonged outages are not yet finalized. Specific terms and a support contact will be published before billing begins. This draft does not establish a no-refund policy.

Nature of information

Content is general information for company research and learning. The service is not intended to provide buy/sell recommendations, trading instructions tailored to individual financial circumstances, custody of client funds, trade execution or brokerage, or guaranteed returns. Third-party analyst targets are identified as their views.

Member Q&A and posts

Questions are shared with PRO members. Selected questions receive answers; not every question is guaranteed an answer. Do not post personal or confidential information, infringing material or abusive content.

Updates and corrections

Source publication, retrieval, translation and connectivity can delay display. Complete news coverage, delivery within a fixed number of seconds and realization of forecasts are not guaranteed. Confirmed errors are intended to be corrected.

Use of content

Use the service for personal research. Do not redistribute articles without permission, publish member-only information, gain unauthorized access or disrupt the service. Final terms, operator identity and liability provisions must be settled before subscriptions open.
