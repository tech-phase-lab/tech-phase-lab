About this page

This draft reflects the service in development. The operator, contact details, retention periods and other outstanding details must be finalized before publication of a final policy.

Information and purposes

The member service is designed to process registration and authentication information such as email addresses, membership plans and expiry dates, submitted questions and content interactions, and notification subscription details for authentication, access management, content delivery, notifications and support. Some preferences, including watchlists and language selection, are stored in the browser.

This overview website

This static overview has no registration or payment forms and no advertising trackers. Its images are records of development screens. The hosting provider may process access logs for delivery and security.

External services

The development service uses Clerk for authentication, Vercel for hosting and Railway for monitoring processes. News translation is designed to use an external AI service with news text as the translation input. A payment provider is not finalized; the actual provider and data processed will be disclosed after selection.

Visibility of submissions

Q&A submissions are visible to PRO members; avoid personal contact details and private financial details. Display-name behavior will be explained before launch.

Management, retention and requests

Access controls and other safeguards are intended. Retention periods, deletion/access/correction request procedures, international processing information and the contact channel are outstanding and must be published with the final operator details before subscriptions open.
