03 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the service now?

The service is currently in development. This page introduces the planned offering. Availability and registration details will be announced when preparations are complete.

Which languages will be available?

We plan to provide the service in English and Japanese, allowing readers to access news summaries and company research in their preferred language.

Will you execute trades or offer personalized investment advice?

We will not hold customer funds, execute or broker trades, or guarantee returns. We plan to provide news and analysis based on public information and a shared member Q&A space, without trading instructions tailored to individual members’ financial circumstances. Analysis and scenarios do not guarantee future outcomes.

What questions will PRO members be able to ask?

We expect questions about interpreting company announcements and earnings, industry developments and similar topics that can be shared with other members. Selected questions will receive answers; an answer to every submission is not guaranteed.

Is every news item delivered within a guaranteed number of seconds?

No complete coverage or delivery time is guaranteed. Source availability, retrieval, translation and connectivity can affect delivery. We are testing acquisition through Japanese and English display before launch.

Can international readers subscribe?

The planned audience includes Japanese and English-speaking readers. US$20 per month is an intended international price. PAY.JP has advised that overseas-issued cards may be submitted for JPY recurring payments; underwriting is not complete. USD billing, supported countries and tax treatment remain to be confirmed.
