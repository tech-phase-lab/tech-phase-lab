# Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Japanese Privacy Policy. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account information: email address, user ID and the information needed to sign in (sign-in is managed by Clerk's service)
- Contract information: plan, contract start date and end date (payment status). We do not receive card numbers or PayPal account details
- RESOLUTE purchase information: purchase date, payment method (PayPal), when the Notification Features expire, your automatic renewal setting, and the payment method identifier issued by PayPal (for automatic renewal). We do not receive the card or bank account numbers themselves
- Settings: display language, notification settings and favorite stocks
- Notification information: device identifiers needed for browser notifications (Web Push) and phone notifications
- Access information: pages viewed, device and browser type, and error records
- Inquiries: the content of your emails
- RESOLUTE diagnosis information: your diagnosis answers and the rules you set (see 2)

## 2. Holdings and Diagnosis Information

Holdings you enter or import (stocks, number of shares, purchase price and so on) are stored on your device and not on the Operator's servers. All confirmed RESOLUTE diagnosis answers and rules (currency and exchange-rate settings, how you buy, conditions for not buying, conditions for stopping, loss-cut figures and so on) are stored on the Operator's servers for each member account. Only you can see them, while signed in and with access to RESOLUTE. You can delete what is stored. When you delete it, it is also deleted from the Operator's servers. It may remain in backups kept for recovery from failures for up to ● days, but it is erased automatically after that period, and even if data is restored from a backup, deleted data and notification settings you turned off are not brought back. Answers in progress (drafts) are stored only in your browser and disappear after a certain time. They are not sent to the server.

## 3. How We Use Information

- To register members and verify their identity
- To bill for the service
- To send emails about prices and renewals
- To improve the service
- Providing the Service and the Product, signing in, managing contracts and purchases (including renewal notices and automatic renewal charges), saving and showing diagnosis results and rules, and notifications based on your rules
- Sending notifications
- Answering inquiries
- Preventing misuse and investigating the causes of failures
- Contacting you about important notices

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in and to save your settings. You can turn them off in your browser settings, but some features may then not work.

## 5. Retention Period

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes of use. Diagnosis answers and rules are kept while you have an account. When you close your account, we delete them, including from backups, within ● days, except for information we must keep by law.

## 6. Requests for Disclosure, Correction and Deletion

We respond to requests from you for disclosure, correction or deletion in accordance with the law. The same applies to requests to notify you of the purposes of use, to stop using or erase data, or to stop providing it to third parties. Please contact the address below to make a request. After confirming your identity, we will explain the procedure. Contact: support@techphase.app

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures such as access restrictions and encrypted communication. Where we handle personal data using the services of businesses in other countries, we take security measures after understanding the personal information protection systems of those countries.

## 8. Changes

We will announce changes to this policy in the Service.

## 9. Use of External Businesses

We use the services of external businesses for sign-in (Clerk), payment and saving your payment method for automatic renewal (PayPal), and sending emails and notifications. Phone and browser notifications (Web Push) are delivered, depending on your device and browser, through Apple Push Notification service (Apple), Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) or Mozilla's push service (Mozilla). We may have these businesses handle your information to the extent necessary. They include businesses in the United States and other countries outside Japan. We do not provide personal data to third parties without your consent, except as permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information.
