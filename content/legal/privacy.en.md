# Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Japanese Privacy Policy. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

The Tech Phase Research Operations Office (the "Operator") handles the personal information it collects through Tech Phase and RESOLUTE (the "Service") as follows.

## 1. Operator

- Name: Tech Phase Research Operations Office
- Operator's name and address: [Disclosed without delay upon request]
- Contact: [Contact email address]

## 2. Information We Collect

1. Information you give us when you register: email address, password (stored in a form that cannot be reversed), and display language
2. Purchase and contract information: the product purchased, the amount, the date and time, and the payment service's transaction number
3. Information you enter in the Service: your watchlist, Stock Notebook notes, research notes, questions to Ask Rizel, and your RESOLUTE diagnostic answers and alert settings
4. Usage records: IP address, browser type, access date and time, and identifiers such as cookies

Payment information such as credit card numbers is entered on the payment service's (PayPal's) screens. The Operator does not collect or store it.

In the RESOLUTE diagnostic, you may be asked to enter amounts such as living expenses and investment amounts. These are used only to create your rules. Photos used for the rules charm are processed only on your device and are not sent to the Operator.

## 3. How We Use Information

1. Providing the Service, verifying identity, and managing logins
2. Billing, confirming payments, and granting or suspending usage rights
3. Sending notification emails such as Closing-price Alerts and Next-morning Notes
4. Responding to inquiries and sending important notices
5. Preventing unauthorized use and ensuring security
6. Analysis to improve the Service (done in a form that does not identify individuals)
7. Informing you about new features and products (you can opt out of these messages)

## 4. Outsourcing to External Services

The Operator outsources work such as payments, servers, and email delivery to outside companies. These companies include businesses in the United States, and personal information is stored on servers in the United States.

## 5. Provision to Third Parties

The Operator does not provide personal information to third parties without the person's consent, except in the following cases.

1. When required by law
2. When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain the person's consent
3. When it is necessary to cooperate with a government agency or similar body carrying out duties prescribed by law
4. When personal information is passed on as a result of a business succession

## 6. Use of Cookies

The Service uses technologies such as cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your display language. [If an analytics tool is used, its name and how to disable it will be written here.] You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features will then be unavailable.

## 7. Security Measures

To prevent leaks, loss, or damage of personal information, the Operator takes the following measures.

1. Encrypting communications (HTTPS)
2. Storing passwords in a form that cannot be reversed
3. Restricting access to the administration screens and using two-step verification
4. Selecting and supervising the companies we outsource to

## 8. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, and Suspension of Use

If a person asks us to disclose, correct, add to, or delete their personal information, to stop using it, or to stop providing it to third parties, we will confirm the person's identity and respond without delay in accordance with the law. Please send requests to the contact above. Even after you withdraw, we keep purchase records that are required for legal or accounting purposes for a certain period.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

The Operator may change this Policy when necessary. We will announce important changes in the Service in advance.
